## 3D modelling method

The detailed quadcopter (`frontend/src/models/detail/`) follows the method of the `cutaway` Claude Code skill,
[Nyxera0x/cutaway](https://github.com/Nyxera0x/cutaway) (MIT), itself built on
[whaleyxbt/claude-motion](https://github.com/whaleyxbt/claude-motion) (MIT): a material "shop" plus a few
shape helpers, profiles turned round an axis with hard edges kept, dense mechanism under plates with gaps, and
a written checklist graded against the build. Its files were read as reference; none of its code, assets,
fonts or sounds are bundled here, and its install and render scripts were not run.
